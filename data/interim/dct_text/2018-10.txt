Updated Hotel Classification System for Emirate’s Hotels Launched by
the Department of Culture and Tourism – Abu Dhabi: The Department
of Culture and Tourism - Abu Dhabi (DCT Abu Dhabi) has unveiled
improvements to its classification system for all hotels in the emirate,
which will apply to all establishments from this point on, and are a
further significant joint step in developing the emirate as a world-class
destination. This process of evolving and improving the classification
system is in direct response to consultation with hotel partners.
