The Department of Culture and Tourism - Abu Dhabi inaugurates
‘Bait Al Gahwa’ : DCT Abu Dhabi has inaugurated the ‘Bait Al
Gahwa’ (Coffee House) initiative at Manarat Al Saadiyat on Saadiyat
Island. Bait Al Gahwa is a key element of DCT Abu Dhabi’s strategy
to revive authentic heritage and traditions. It aims to provide
licenses for operating tourism projects that offer a real-life
simulation of the traditional Emirati process of preparing and
serving coffee.
