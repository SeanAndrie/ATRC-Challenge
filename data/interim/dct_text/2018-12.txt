Landmark Exhibition of Emirati artist Abdul Qader Al Rais opens in
Abu Dhabi: The Department opened a major retrospective exhibition
of artworks by the celebrated Emirati painter Abdul Qader Al Rais this
month, showcasing his works from the late 1960s up until the present
day. “Abdul Qader Al Rais: 50 Years of Art” surveys the evolution of Al
Rais’ career, from his early figurative paintings to his most recent and
recognisable series of calligraphic abstraction. The exhibition runs at
Manarat Al Saadiyat until 23rd March, 2019.
