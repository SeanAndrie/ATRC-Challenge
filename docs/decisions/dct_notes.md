# DCT Abu Dhabi monthly hotel report parse notes

Generated by `pipeline/dct/dct_parse.py` from the cached `pdftotext -layout`
text of all 99 PDFs in `data/raw/dct_pdfs/`. No values are estimated or
interpolated: anything not printed unambiguously in a report is left out of the CSVs.

> Updated during the 2026-10 repository reorganisation. The substance below is
> unchanged; only the paths are current. One caveat has since been added:
> `derived == 1` rows for 2017-11 through 2021-03 are bar-chart readings, so
> "no values are estimated" holds for the printed tables but not for those
> chart-derived rows. See `docs/data_dictionary.md`.

## Validation performed

Run `pipeline/dct/dct_validate.py` to reproduce the reconciliation below.

- **2017 guests reconcile against the published annual total.** The twelve monthly
  values sum to 4,874,253 against the 4,875,499 stated in the December 2017
  report, a difference of 1,246 (0.026%). This is the strongest single check in
  the pipeline: it confirms each month is paired with the right metric rather
  than merely being present.
- **2024 reconciles exactly.** The twelve monthly guest values sum to 5,819K,
  exactly the annual total printed beside them (0.000%).
- Every chart-era file cross-checks the three-region guest/revenue sums against
  the KPI chart baseline: 32/32 checks inside tolerance, 0 mismatches.
- 2019 quarterly guest totals cross-check against the sum of the three months of
  the monthly chart in the same report (Q1 1,291,482 / Q2 1,194,620 /
  Q3 1,311,551 / Q4 1,337,777).
- Occupancy is within 0-100 and ALOS within 0.5-12 for every parsed row.
- Nationality rows are de-duplicated on (file, granularity, market).
- YTD continuity within 2023 holds: YTD Aug 3,246K + Sep 408K = YTD Sep 3,654K,
  and + Oct 424K = YTD Oct 4,078K, matching the printed YTD figures.

## Unit normalisation

The reports change unit between eras, so `dct_validate.py` converts every row to
absolute counts before reconciling. Summing the raw `value` column across years
is meaningless:

| years | printed unit |
| --- | --- |
| 2015-2018, 2020, 2021 (Jan-Mar) | `count` |
| 2018-01, 2021-04..12, 2019, 2022-2024 | `thousands` |
| 2024 headline prose | `count` (5.8 million) |

2018 and 2021 legitimately mix `count` and `thousands` between months; the
values agree once converted, so both are kept.

## Known gaps and deliberate rejections

- **February 2015 and February 2018 are both unrecoverable.** One bad manifest
  link cached the February 2017 `Hotel Report` twice, as `2015-02.txt` and
  `2018-02.txt`; the two files are byte-identical (34,813 chars) and both declare
  `period_head=2017-02`. `dct_parse.py` now hashes every cached text file and
  skips byte-identical copies, so neither stem contributes rows. Previously the
  duplicate injected a phantom 2018-02 row (the regional sum of a 2017 document)
  and gave 2018 a second February, inflating that year by 405,849. Both years are
  now correctly short one month: 2015 sums to 3,798,072 against a printed annual
  4,105,846, and 2018 to 4,653,773 across eleven months.
- The `text_fingerprint.json` `period_head` field is **not** a safe validity gate.
  It reads the prior-year comparison heading, so it labels the 2017 reports as
  2016 and the 2019 quarters as `2019-??`. Only byte-identical hashing is used to
  detect misfiled files.
- **2023 is short November and December.** Its ten months sum to 4,065K against
  a prior-year total of 5,504K printed in the 2024 report, a 26% gap consistent
  with two missing high-season months. That same 2024 report prints a full
  twelve-month 2023 series, but it sums to 5,504K while 2023's own monthly
  reports give 4,065K for January to October, so it is a restatement on a
  different basis and is deliberately not merged; the conflict is recorded in
  `dct_notes_raw.csv`.
- Some chart-era reports print a two-group `KEY INDICATORS` block that is
  `ABU DHABI ISLAND PERFORMANCE`, not an emirate total. Those blocks are ignored;
  emirate KPIs come from the three-region sum instead.
- Chart-era chart baselines for 2017-11/2017-12 are internally inconsistent, so
  rate metrics are only emitted when they pass the plausibility ranges.
- `TOP NON-UAE NATIONALITIES` in the 2020-2021 chart era, and 2017-11/2017-12,
  are laid out as interleaved column blocks whose guest values cannot be paired
  with the printed change percentages; only rows whose current/prior pair
  reproduces the printed change within 3.5 points are kept, so those files
  contribute few or no nationality rows.
- The 2017-12 report states December guests as 490,845 in prose while the KPI
  chart parses as 490,884, a 39-guest disagreement; the chart value is used.
- 2019-Q3/Q4 and 2017 chart-era ALOS could not be derived reliably; left null.
- Hotel and room inventory is not printed in the 2015-2016 or 2021-04..2023-10
  reports, and Al Ain room counts are missing from the 2020/2021 regional supply
  tables, so those cells are absent rather than guessed.
- 2024 `ALOS`: the narrative headline (3.2) disagrees with the annual trend table;
  the trend-table value is used and the conflict is flagged here.
- Region/class supply is not additive across classes for every report, so only
  printed rows are emitted; no class values are summed into a total.
- The 2015-12 report carries the Main Indicators Summary twice, once scoped to
  December and once to the full year, and both blocks emit the same twelve
  metrics. `fix_december_annual` separates them by document order, because the
  current-month block is parsed first. It previously used a magnitude test
  (`year >= 3 * month`) with a whitelist of additive units, which worked for
  counts and currency totals but could never work for a rate or an average where
  the annual figure is close to the monthly one. That left `alos` (2.98 instead
  of 3.49), `arr` (438 instead of 462), `occupancy_rate` (75.4 instead of 77.0)
  and `revpar` (330 instead of 356) filing their full-year average as a
  December monthly reading. The four rows are now `granularity=year`. The
  consequence was not cosmetic: `guest_nights = hotel_guests * alos` reproduces
  DCT's own published guest nights to within 0.174 per cent across the 21 months
  where DCT prints it, which is the identity the chain's terminal node rests on.
  The rule is scoped to `granularity=month` so the 2024 current/prior-year pairs,
  which are already annual and also two-row groups, are left alone.

## Schema notes

- `unit` is one of `count`, `thousands`, `aed`, `aed_million`, `pct`, `days`.
  `millions` is no longer emitted: the only prose value that carried it, the
  2024 headline of 5.8 million guests, is now stored as `count` with the same
  absolute magnitude, so `unit` no longer contradicts `value`.
- `granularity` is `month`, `ytd`, `quarter` or `year`; `ytd=1` marks a printed or
  derived cumulative row and `derived=1` marks a value computed from other
  printed values in the same report (for example a three-region sum).
- A cumulative figure is never filed as a month. In the key-indicator era the
  YTD table is found by its six-column header rather than by its heading, because
  2023-09 titles its monthly table `TOP HOTEL GUEST NATIONALITIES` and keeps
  `OVERALL PERFORMANCE` only on the YTD table, which had caused a YTD total of
  3,654K to be written as September's 408K month. The `YTD` marker is read from
  the lines immediately around the header.
- `period` is `MM` for months, `Q1`-`Q4` for quarters and `annual` for the 2024
  annual report.
- Nationality `guests_unit` is `count` for 2015-2016 and `thousands` where the
  source table prints `(000)`.
