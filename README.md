# ATRC Challenge — Abu Dhabi hotel demand and air arrivals

Asking whether air passenger arrivals can stand in for hotel demand in Abu
Dhabi, and what follows if they cannot.

The short answer, from `docs/aggregate_link_test.md`: **no.** A seasonal and
trend model that never sees arrivals scores a 5.2% WMAPE on held-out years.
Feeding it arrivals pushes that to 12.6%. The elasticity of hotel guests to
arrivals is 0.170 with a standard error of 0.176 — indistinguishable from zero.

## Reading the results

Start with `docs/data_dictionary.md`. In particular:

- **`dct_kpis.csv` has a `unit` column and it changes mid-series.** Guest counts
  are raw `count` through 2021-03 and `thousands` from 2021-04. Summing across
  that boundary without normalising looks like catastrophic data loss.
- **68 rows are `derived == 1`,** mostly bar-chart readings for 2017-11 through
  2021-03 rather than printed figures.
- **ADS-B captures only 48.9-61.5% of official arrivals.** Use it for shares,
  mix and direction — never quote raw ADS-B volumes as demand levels.
- **203 ADS-B legs in 2025 Q1 are `is_local_leg`** — domestic repositioning, not
  inbound demand. `test_market_link.py` filters them; any new consumer must too.
- **Prefer `granularity == 'month'`.** Nothing consumes the `ytd` grain, and it
  carries the historical unit defects listed in the dictionary.

## Where the analysis stands

Established in `docs/flight_hotel_link_findings.md` and the three generated test
reports:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do arrivals predict hotel guests? | Not beyond seasonality. Elasticity 0.170 (se 0.176). |
| Market-level or aggregate? | Aggregate only, and weakly. Market-level conversion spreads 36x across 9 markets. |
| Are hotel guests internally consistent? | Yes. `guests x ALOS = guest_nights` to within 0.174%. |
| Is there a true international series? | Only 2024, and it comes from narrative prose. |
| Can the ex-UAE proxy replace it? | No. It overstates true international share by 12.8, 20.3 and 27.6 points in 2021-2023. |

## Open work

- **Simulator.** A marimo model over origin markets, built on the market-level
  conversion spread rather than a single blended ratio.
- **52 air-only origins** have no measured hotel capture rate. OpenSky data in
  `data/raw/opensky/` is intended to close this and is not yet consumed.
- **VFR share is unmeasured and no public source exists.** Any
  passenger-to-visitor conversion is therefore an explicit assumption, not an
  estimate. Do not let a simulator imply otherwise.
- **Retargeting** does not recover a reliable international relationship.
